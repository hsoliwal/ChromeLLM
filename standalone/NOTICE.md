# Removed installed-Chrome provider

Copyright 2026 Hitesh Soliwal and Contributors to the Synexia Project.

The installed-Chrome AI provider (`ChromeAiBackend`, `ChromeDevTools`, `ChromeProcess`, `ChromeAiScript`
and their contract tests) was removed on 2026-11-17. Its Apache-2.0 notices are preserved in Git history:
the adapted Synexia CDP transport, and the browser-side session lifecycle adapted from Google's
[web-ai-demos](https://github.com/GoogleChromeLabs/web-ai-demos/tree/2edcaedca4a13913598b6365d3c41b0f96e8d09d/easy-language-model)
(Copyright 2026 Google LLC, Apache License 2.0). No code from those sources remains in this module.
The repository's historical Chrome2api attribution and MIT license remain in LICENSE, NOTICE and THIRD_PARTY.md.
